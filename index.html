What We Do

Nepal's Most Trusted Air Cargo GSSA Company

A General Sales & Service Agent (GSSA) is a company appointed by an airline to act as its exclusive cargo sales and operations representative in a defined territory. The GSSA manages all aspects of cargo sales, reservations, customer service, marketing, and regulatory compliance — allowing carriers to maximise cargo revenue without the cost of a direct office.

Starlight Express is the GSSA of choice for international airlines in Nepal. With 30 years of market expertise, Nepal's first ISO 9001 certification in international freight, and the global backing of Kales Airline Services, we position our airline partners to capture maximum air cargo revenue from Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM).

Air Cargo GSSA in Nepal — FAQ

Quick answers about Starlight Express, our GSSA services, and air cargo out of Nepal.

What is a GSSA?

A General Sales & Service Agent (GSSA) is a company appointed by an airline to act as its exclusive cargo sales and operations representative in a defined territory. The GSSA handles all aspects of cargo bookings, sales, customer service, marketing, and regulatory compliance on behalf of the airline.

Who is the best air cargo GSSA in Nepal?

Starlight Express Pvt. Ltd. is widely recognised as Nepal's leading air cargo GSSA. Established in April 1995, ISO 9001:2015 certified, and strategically partnered with Kales Airline Services, Starlight Express represents multiple international airlines for air cargo out of Kathmandu (KTM).

How do I track an air cargo shipment with Starlight Express?

Enter your AWB (Air Waybill) number on our tracking page. Real-time tracking is powered by CargoMIS — the integrated cargo management information system used across the air cargo industry. AWB format: 3-digit airline prefix + 8 digits (e.g. 235-12345678).
