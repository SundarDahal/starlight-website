Our GSSA Services

End-to-end air cargo General Sales & Service Agent solutions for international airlines in Nepal

Core GSSA Services

Full-Spectrum Air Cargo Representation

Starlight Express provides a complete suite of GSSA services, enabling airline partners to maximise cargo revenue in Nepal without the overhead of a direct presence.

Cargo Sales Agency

We act as the exclusive sales arm for our airline principals in Nepal — marketing cargo products, managing accounts, setting tariffs, and driving revenue growth across the freight forwarding community in Kathmandu and beyond.

Revenue & Yield Management

Expert space allotment, pricing strategy, and revenue optimisation. We ensure that every kilogram of capacity on our airline partners' flights generates maximum yield, using data-driven approaches backed by Kales' global expertise.

Reservations & Space Management

Full management of cargo reservations, booking systems, and capacity allocation. We ensure smooth space management from booking through to uplift, minimising offloads and maximising load factors.

Special Cargo Handling

Expert management of temperature-controlled pharmaceuticals (GDP-compliant), perishables, live animals, dangerous goods (IATA-DGR compliant), high-value, and oversized cargo requiring special attention and documentation.

Agent Network Management

We develop and manage relationships with Nepal's freight forwarding, import-export, and logistics community on behalf of our airline principals — ensuring consistent performance standards and strong commercial relationships.

Regulatory & Customs Compliance

Full compliance management with Nepal's Civil Aviation Authority (CAAN), customs regulations, IATA standards, and bilateral air service agreements — ensuring every cargo movement is legally sound and efficiently processed.

Marketing & Communications

Local marketing, promotional campaigns, industry event representation, and communications on behalf of our airline partners — building brand awareness and commercial presence in the Nepal market.

Tracking & Reporting

Integrated shipment tracking through CargoMIS, combined with comprehensive performance reporting and analytics for airline principals — providing full transparency on cargo volumes, revenue, and market trends.

Ground Handling Coordination

Coordination with ground handling agents at Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) to ensure smooth cargo acceptance, build-up, loading, and documentation for all inbound and outbound shipments.
